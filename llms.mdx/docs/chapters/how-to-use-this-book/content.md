# How to use this book (/docs/chapters/how-to-use-this-book)



## Start where you are [#start-where-you-are]

You don't have to read this book from front to back before you can do anything with it. Start with the part that fits your life right now, whether you have income and debt, have no income coming in, or are trying to work out what to do with the money you have.

If you're unsure where to begin, [Chapter 1: Start where you are](/docs/chapters/start-where-you-are) will help you put your situation on paper. From there, it gives you one next chapter to use. That way, you've got somewhere to start without having to work through every topic first.

## Put it to work [#put-it-to-work]

As you go through a section, read the explanation and work through the example before trying it with your own numbers. The example helps you see how the idea works; the exercise is where you find out what it means for your life.

The book explains each step, and the [companion workbook](/docs/workbook) gives you space to write your answers. When I say “go to workbook page 1,” that's where you'll find the matching exercise.

If you don't have the companion workbook, a notebook or plain paper is fine. Each exercise tells you what to write, so you can do every step without buying anything else. You can also use a document you keep beside you if that works better than writing by hand.

You're probably not going to remember every single thing you read in here, and you don't need to. Come back when you get paid, when you're thinking about a purchase, or when your situation changes and you need to work through a different problem. The reader-group labels from Chapter 1 will help you find the steps and notes that fit.

## Work toward your own life [#work-toward-your-own-life]

Keep the life you want in mind as you use the book. You might want to travel more, or you might want a job you enjoy that lets you come home and have dinner with your family every night. Those are different goals, and they'll ask different things of your money.

[Chapter 2: The life you want](/docs/chapters/the-life-you-want) helps you describe that life and put a cost on it. Then we can work backward to the choices you can make from where you are.
