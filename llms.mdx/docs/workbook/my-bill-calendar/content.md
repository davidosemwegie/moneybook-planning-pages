Running amount = previous amount + money in − money out or newly reserved. A forecast receipt stays marked expected until it arrives. If it is late, recalculate.

A reservation is counted once: don't subtract it again when paying from that reserved money. This calendar tracks money left for this plan, which can differ from the balance shown by your bank. Transfers between included accounts don't change their combined balance. Keep them labeled rather than counting them as new income or duplicate spending.
