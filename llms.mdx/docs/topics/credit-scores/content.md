## Questions from the plan [#questions-from-the-plan]

* What is a credit score?
* When was it created?
* Does it matter, and in which situations?

## Research before drafting [#research-before-drafting]

Choose the country and scoring system before writing the history or explaining the factors. Different systems should not be treated as one universal score.

Use primary sources to explain where a score is used and what the reader can learn from it. Keep score questions distinct from the [credit card](/docs/topics/credit-cards) and [debt](/docs/topics/debt) explanations.

## Exercise to build [#exercise-to-build]

Create a task that helps the reader understand their own credit information. The exact steps and links depend on the selected country.
