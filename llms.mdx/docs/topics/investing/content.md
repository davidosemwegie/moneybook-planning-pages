## What to cover [#what-to-cover]

The written plan includes stocks, real estate, crypto, and investing in businesses. Each needs a plain explanation, an example, and an account of how money can be gained or lost.

[Note 3](/docs/raw-thoughts/note-3) also asks about index funds, bonds, Treasuries, private equity, and hedge funds. Chapter 12 includes these within its sections, with private equity and hedge funds as reference material.

## Connect it to the reader's situation [#connect-it-to-the-readers-situation]

The timing of this topic depends on the reader's income, debt, goals, and circumstances. Build and research those connections before writing a rule about when everyone should invest.
