# Companion workbook (/docs/workbook)



The book walks you through each step. This workbook gives you a place to put your answers.

When a chapter sends you to a workbook page, fill it out here. Keep your pages so you can come back to them when your life changes.

**Don't have the companion workbook?** A notebook or plain paper works too. The book tells you what to write. You can do every exercise without the workbook. You can also use a document if writing by hand doesn't work for you.

Write what you know. Leave details blank or write “not sure yet” when you need to come back to them. An unknown amount isn't zero.
