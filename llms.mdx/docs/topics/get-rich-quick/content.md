# Get-rich-quick schemes (/docs/topics/get-rich-quick)



**Chapter destination:** [10. The promise of fast money](/docs/chapters/fast-money). These are supporting notes for the manuscript.

## Working angle [#working-angle]

The [general plan](/docs/raw-thoughts/general-plan) calls for explaining why get-rich-quick schemes do not work.

Choose specific examples and explain what is being promised, where the money comes from, and what evidence supports the outcome being advertised. A broad claim needs a clear definition of the schemes covered.

## Connection to the book [#connection-to-the-book]

[Notes 1 and 2](/docs/raw-thoughts) explain that the author is not promising to make every reader a millionaire. This section can connect that stance to the reader's own goals and the work needed to reach them.

## Exercise to build [#exercise-to-build]

Give the reader an example offer to examine. Ask them to identify the promise, cost, assumptions, and evidence. Use sourced cases before writing the final explanation.
