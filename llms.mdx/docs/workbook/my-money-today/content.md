Put the pen down and look at the page. Read each total and the gaps you've marked. This is what you know today. You can keep going with a few boxes left blank.

Keep these totals separate. Money on hand, a month's income, and debt each tell you something different. Don't add them into one total.

Keep the sheet and update it when things change.
