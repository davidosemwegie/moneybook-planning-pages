## Build around the starting situation [#build-around-the-starting-situation]

I-D and N-D need distinct guidance. In the recording, the author puts finding income first for someone who has none. That idea needs a researched sequence which also accounts for bills and obligations that cannot wait.

The author defines debt as money owed. The [Chapter 1 map](/docs/chapters/start-where-you-are#what-counts-as-debt) includes borrowed money, unpaid bills, and taxes, fines, and court-ordered amounts. The author's target is income without debt; the repayment sequence still needs evidence and context.
