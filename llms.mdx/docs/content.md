## Add another recording [#add-another-recording]

Attach it in the Codex chat for this project. The assistant will add the next numbered raw note and place its ideas in the book. The [workflow](/docs/writing-plan) explains how the transcript, draft prose, and facts to check fit together.

The book title is still open. The [brief](/docs/book-brief) records the promise being developed, and [Research notes](/docs/research) tracks the evidence still needed.
