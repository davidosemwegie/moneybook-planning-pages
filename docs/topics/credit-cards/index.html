Credit cards 101

Explain interest, minimum payments, and the tradeoffs of using a card.

Chapter destination: 6. Credit cards 101. The destination chapter now has a first draft and a spoken adaptation. These remain supporting source notes; the chapter holds the developed exercise and scoped research.

The reader's question

How does a credit card work, and what can go wrong?

The plan calls credit cards a double-edged sword. The chapter needs to explain the useful parts and the risks with a worked example.

What to cover

  • Interest and the meaning of an annual interest rate.
  • Minimum payments and what they do to the balance.
  • The difference between using credit and carrying debt.
  • When the author would use a credit card or a debit card.

Use an actual card agreement or official guidance for the selected country. Define the example's rate, fees, payment timing, and assumptions.

Exercise to build

Have the reader find the relevant numbers on their own statement, then walk through a payment decision. Add notes for I-D and N-D where the situation changes the next step.
