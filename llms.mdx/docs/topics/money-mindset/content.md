**Chapter destination:** [2. The life you want](/docs/chapters/the-life-you-want). These are supporting notes for the manuscript.

## The point from the recordings [#the-point-from-the-recordings]

People want different things from money. In [Note 1](/docs/raw-thoughts/note-1), the author names security, travel, cars, watches, and medical bills for family. He does not want people shamed for choosing a different goal.

[Note 2](/docs/raw-thoughts/note-2) makes room for changes over time. Someone's current situation does not describe the rest of their life.

## Draft passage [#draft-passage]

You might want to travel. You might want to know your bills are covered. You might want to be home for dinner with your family.

Start there. What do you want your money to help you do?

The answer does not have to match anyone else's.

## Develop next [#develop-next]

Connect this stance to the [life you want exercise](/docs/topics/dream-lifestyle). The broader claim that mindset affects money decisions needs examples and evidence. Keep the reader's circumstances in view; avoid implying that a change in attitude alone resolves a money problem.
