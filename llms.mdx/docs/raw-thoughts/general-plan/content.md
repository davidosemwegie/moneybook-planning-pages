# General plan (/docs/raw-thoughts/general-plan)



These are the supplied planning notes, lightly cleaned for spelling and formatting. Open blanks and conflicting labels are retained. The [book plan](/docs/outline) develops them using the recordings.

## The book [#the-book]

This book is not meant to be read front to back. It's a workbook that gives you a practical step-by-step guide on how to \_\_\_\_\_.

Notes:

* Extremely practical guide.
* Backed with facts.
* Stories / examples.

## We will teach people about [#we-will-teach-people-about]

* What should you do when you get paid? How to split up a pay cheque.
* Why get-rich-quick schemes do not work.
* Everyone's financial journey is different and people have different priorities.
* Not everyone is meant to be a business owner or a full-time employee.
* Credit Card 101: interest, minimum payments, and why credit cards are a double-edged sword.
* Debt 101: how to get out of debt, not get into debt, control and manage debt, and balance payments.
* Increasing your monthly income is the number-one priority.
* How to make a decision if you want to buy something expensive.
* Why budgeting apps don't really help you.
* Your mentality about money truly does matter.
* Should you use BNPL apps?
* Investing 101: stocks, real estate, crypto, and investing in businesses.
* Credit Score 101: what it is, when it was created, and whether / when it matters.
* An exercise to figure out how much money you need for your dream lifestyle. Sit down and calculate exactly how much you need.

Each group will have an ID. Each topic will have bullet points or key material meant for the different groups.

Qualifications listed: you don't have a job, or you get paid hourly.

Paths: if you have any debt, start here; if you don't have debt, start here.

Proposed ID: triangle / Hourly.

Qualification: you earn a salary.

Paths: if you have debt, start here; if you don't have any debt, start here.

## Writing style [#writing-style]

The supplied style direction names a third-grade reading target and fifth-grade maximum, drawing on Alex Hormozi's writing guidelines.

* Use present voice and simple tenses.
* Write in active voice.
* Delete adverbs and use strong, specific verbs.
* Keep sentences short. Aim for a maximum of one comma.
* Choose basic, everyday words.

Supplied references: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/H-MXJIUkSi8), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@ahormozi/video/7242370736713764139), and [Skool](https://www.skool.com/acquisitionuniversity/alex-hormozis-writing-guidelines-prompt-gpt).

The author also asks for the writing to match the way he speaks in the raw recordings.
