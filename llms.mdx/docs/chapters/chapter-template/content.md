# Chapter template (/docs/chapters/chapter-template)



## The reader's question [#the-readers-question]

State the decision this section helps the reader make.

## The explanation [#the-explanation]

Give the reader the concept in plain words. Define any financial term on first use. Follow the [writing guide](/docs/writing-style).

## A story or worked example [#a-story-or-worked-example]

Use an experience from a source note or a clearly labelled example. Keep assumptions, amounts, and currencies visible. Do not invent an event from the author's life.

## What to do [#what-to-do]

Write the steps in order. Make each one specific enough to carry out.

## Notes for each reader group [#notes-for-each-reader-group]

Use the [reader IDs](/docs/reader-paths) for steps that change with income type or debt. Shared guidance applies to all readers.

## Your exercise [#your-exercise]

Give the reader a task with a clear result: a number, a list, a plan, or a decision.

Keep the explanation and worked example in the book. Put blank tables and answer spaces on a named, numbered page in the companion workbook. Link to that page from the exercise. Give the same prompts in plain words so a reader without the workbook can complete every step on paper. Keep unknown details optional where the chapter has not explained them yet.

## Where to go next [#where-to-go-next]

Give each relevant reader path one next chapter. Keep the next step clear.

## Source notes and facts to check [#source-notes-and-facts-to-check]

Link to the original Raw thoughts page. Add primary sources for factual claims and record unresolved questions in [Research notes](/docs/research).
