# Buying something expensive (/docs/topics/big-purchases)



**Chapter destination:** [8. Before you buy](/docs/chapters/before-you-buy). The destination chapter now has a first draft and a spoken adaptation. These remain supporting source notes; the chapter holds the developed exercise and scoped research.

## The reader's question [#the-readers-question]

How do I decide whether to buy something expensive?

The book should give the reader a process they can repeat. It needs to connect the purchase to what they want from life, what it costs, and their current income and debt.

## Exercise to build [#exercise-to-build]

Ask the reader to name the purchase and explain what it adds to their life. Then build a worked comparison of the available choices and their full costs. The final result should be a decision with reasons.

Use the [life you want exercise](/docs/topics/dream-lifestyle) for context. Put financing details in [Debt 101](/docs/topics/debt) and [Buy now, pay later](/docs/topics/bnpl).
